Marble Lab

🟠 Welcome to Marble Lab!

This is a physics playground inspired by the YouTuber MIKAN, who makes marble-race and survival videos. Marbles can't drive themselves — they only move because of gravity, bouncing, and friction, just like real life.

Quick start

  1. Pick a level on the left.
  2. Press ▶ Play (or the Space bar). Watch the 3-2-1 countdown, then GO!
  3. Press ↻ Reset to run it again — physics is a little different every time.

The three game modes

  • 🏁 Race — first marble across the green finish line wins.
  • 🌋 Survival — lava rises from the bottom; the last marble alive wins.
  • 🏆 Elimination — every few seconds the marble in last place is out.

Be a scientist 🔬

Use the sliders to run experiments. Make a prediction first! “If I turn gravity down, will the marbles bounce higher or lower? Will the race take longer?” Then test it. Try bounciness at 0 vs 0.9, or friction at 0 (ice!) vs 1 (sticky).

Build your own 🛠️

Pick a build tool, then drag on the play area to draw walls, ramps, spinners and the finish line, or click to drop marbles or toggle lava. Use Erase to remove one thing, or Clear everything to start blank.

Make a video like MIKAN 🎥

  1. Press ⛶ Fullscreen for a clean, big picture.
  2. Turn on Trails and Leaderboard for that satisfying look.
  3. Screen-record your computer: Windows = Win+G (Game Bar), Mac = Shift+Cmd+5.
  4. Press Play, let the marbles race, and stop recording at the winner banner!
